The eigenvalue example was measured with ManimGX 0.1.1 and Noto Sans. Native wide (960×540) and portrait (540×960) runs each inspected 145 object-ID frames and produced 145 normal-color pixel callbacks at 24 fps. Each dual pass charged 150,336,000 pixels. The source examples include five keyframes for each of three objects. Eigenvalues are exact affine branches; determinant values are exact only at their five samples and linearly interpolated between them. Native GPU checks are separate from hosted dependency-free tests. Hosted CI does not certify a GPU backend or replay the native rendering automatically.

No universal reverse engineering, new mathematical theory, speedup over another renderer, outside adoption or security advisory credit is claimed.
