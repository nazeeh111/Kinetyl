# Launch film

The 15.6-second film shows the curved-crossing example at 3.00 s before and after layout compilation, followed by 4.2 s of existing native-output details played at normal speed. The paths and event time are unchanged; label placement changes.

Browser SVG states come from committed source [2363aea](https://github.com/nazeeh111/Kinetyl/tree/2363aea4f87c38c79986b93eed2340df136411a7). The subsequent recovery-control patch does not change those drawings. The native details crop the existing [wide](native/curved-wide.mp4) and [portrait](native/curved-portrait.mp4) exports from 1.0–5.2 s; their original receipts remain alongside the videos. This film is an edited explanation, not a new native render or another verification result.

Declared browser geometry and sampled native-pixel checks retain their separate scope. The selected comparison poster is baked into frame zero. The final H.264/AAC render contains 468 frames at 30 fps and passed complete decoding and sampled decoded-frame inspection.

Two quiet selection sounds use Kenney's [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), distributed under CC0. No music or narration. The project graphics and original native outputs retain their existing provenance and licenses.
